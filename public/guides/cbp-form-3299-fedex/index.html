CBP Form 3299 for FedEx Shipments: How to Clear Unaccompanied Baggage & Avoid Holds

By Customs Documentation Editorial Team · Reviewed by CBP Compliance Specialists · Updated October 2026

If your FedEx international tracking status suddenly reads "Clearance Delay - Import" or "Shipment requires a clearance document: CBP Form 3299 required," your parcel has been intercepted at a U.S. Customs and Border Protection port facility.

Every week, thousands of travelers, relocating professionals, students, and expats send suitcases, personal effects, or household goods via FedEx International Priority or Economy. Because these goods travel separately from your passenger flight, U.S. law classifies them as unaccompanied articles under 19 CFR § 148.52.

FedEx cannot legally clear personal belongings through customs on a standard commercial manifest. To release your shipment from bonded storage at major hub facilities like Memphis (MEM) or Indianapolis (IND), FedEx Express customs brokers must present a completed, signed CBP Form 3299 to federal officers on your behalf.

2. Why Waste Hours Dealing with FedEx Brokerage Holds?

When FedEx freezes your package, the clock immediately begins ticking:

  • The 3-Day Demurrage Deadline: FedEx provides a brief complimentary grace period (typically 3 to 5 business days). Once that passes, your package is moved to a bonded Container Freight Station (CFS), racking up daily storage charges between $25 and $100+ per day.
  • The Frustration of Email Loops: Back-and-forth email exchanges with FedEx Trade Networks (ftn_inbound@fedex.com) can take days if your PDF has missing fields, incomplete boxes, or unitemized descriptions.
  • The Risk of Return-to-Origin (RTO): If documents are not submitted within 14 to 30 calendar days, FedEx returns the shipment abroad at the sender's expense—often costing hundreds of dollars in return shipping.

3. Completing CBP Form 3299 Specifically for FedEx

Part I: Importer & Courier Flight Information

  • Box 1 (Name of Importer): Enter your full legal name as it appears in your passport. This must match the Recipient name on the FedEx shipping label.
  • Box 3 (Date of Arrival): Enter the date you physically arrived in the United States. (Note: FedEx cannot clear goods duty-free until you touch down on U.S. soil).
  • Box 4 (U.S. Address): The destination delivery address where FedEx will drop off your package.
  • Box 7 (U.S. Port of Arrival): Enter the international airport where you arrived, or the FedEx entry hub (e.g., Memphis, TN - Port Code 2006 or Indianapolis, IN - Port Code 4110).
  • Box 8 (Name of Arriving Vessel/Carrier and Flight/Voyage #): State: "Federal Express / Tracking # [Your 12-digit FedEx Air Waybill number]". Example: Federal Express / AWB # 7839 2819 0291.
  • Box 9 (Residency Info): Provide your Social Security Number (SSN) if a U.S. citizen/resident, or passport number and issuing country if a foreign national.

Part II: Status of Arriving Person

  • Box A (Returning Resident): Check if you are an American citizen or green card holder returning from an overseas stay. State your dates abroad.
  • Box B (Nonresident): Check if you are entering on a work visa (H-1B, L-1, O-1), student visa (F-1), or permanent immigrant visa.

4. The 4-Part FedEx Clearance Packet

  1. Signed CBP Form 3299 (Rev. 05/24): Complete and signed.
  2. Passport ID Page + U.S. Visa / I-94: Proves legal identity and legal presence in the U.S.
  3. Flight Ticket / Boarding Pass: Confirms you traveled into the U.S. from abroad.
  4. Itemized Baggage Inventory: Clear list of items with estimated USD values. (Auto-generated when using cbpform3299.com).

5. How to Submit Your Documents to FedEx

Reply directly to the clearance notification email from @fedex.com or @ftn.fedex.com. Format your subject line as:
AWB # [Your 12-digit Tracking Number] - CBP Form 3299 Clearance Packet.
