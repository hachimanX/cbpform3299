What the form collects

The form collects only what you type or select:

  • your name (last name, first name, and middle initial) and date of birth
  • a U.S. address
  • travel details: date of arrival, port of arrival, and the vessel or flight
  • names of household members you enter
  • shipment details: date the goods arrive, carrier, country the goods are sent from, bill of lading or air waybill number, number of containers, and marks and numbers
  • the residency category you select, the country you enter, and any length of stay you enter
  • dates you enter if you mark the personnel or evacuee section
  • the article boxes you mark, and any item description, value, and place and date acquired
  • whether you sign as the importer or as an agent, the date, and an agent or firm name if you type one

The form does not ask for a passport number or a Social Security number.

Those answers stay in your browser. They are sent to the payment check only for the moment it takes to build the PDF.

We do not store those answers on a server.
