# CBP Form 3299 for FedEx Shipments: How to Clear Unaccompanied Baggage and Avoid Customs Holds

If your FedEx international tracking status suddenly reads **"Clearance Delay - Import"** or **"Shipment requires a clearance document: CBP Form 3299 required,"** your package has been intercepted at a U.S. Customs and Border Protection port facility.

Every week, thousands of travelers, relocating professionals, students, and expats send suitcases, personal belongings, or household goods via FedEx International Priority or Economy. Because these goods travel separately from your passenger flight, U.S. law classifies them as **unaccompanied articles** under **19 CFR § 148.52**.

FedEx cannot legally clear personal belongings through customs on a standard commercial manifest. To release your shipment from bonded storage at major hub facilities like Memphis (MEM) or Indianapolis (IND), FedEx Express customs brokers must present a completed, signed **CBP Form 3299** to federal officers on your behalf.

When FedEx freezes your package, the clock immediately begins ticking:

1. **The 3-Day Demurrage Deadline:** FedEx provides a brief complimentary grace period (typically 3 to 5 business days). Once that passes, your package is moved to a bonded Container Freight Station (CFS), racking up daily storage charges between **$25 and $100+ per day**.
2. **The Stress of Bureaucratic Communication:** Back-and-forth emails with FedEx Trade Networks (`ftn_inbound@fedex.com`) can take days if your PDF has missing fields or unitemized descriptions.
3. **The Risk of Return-to-Origin (RTO):** If documents are not submitted within 14 to 30 calendar days, FedEx returns the shipment abroad at the sender's expense—often costing hundreds of dollars in return freight.

## 4. Completing CBP Form 3299 Specifically for FedEx

Filing Form 3299 for an express courier differs from filling it out for a maritime container. Follow these specific field instructions to ensure FedEx processes your entry without delays:

### Part I: Importer & Courier Flight Information
* **Box 1 (Name of Importer):** Enter your full legal name as it appears in your passport. This must match the **Recipient** name on the FedEx shipping label.
* **Box 3 (Date of Arrival):** Enter the date you physically arrived in the United States. *(Note: FedEx cannot clear goods duty-free until you touch down on U.S. soil).*
* **Box 4 (U.S. Address):** The destination delivery address where FedEx will drop off your package.
* **Box 7 (U.S. Port of Arrival):** Enter the international airport where you arrived, or the FedEx entry hub (e.g., *Memphis, TN - Port Code 2006* or *Indianapolis, IN - Port Code 4110*).
* **Box 8 (Name of Arriving Vessel/Carrier and Flight/Voyage #):** State: **"Federal Express / Tracking # [Your 12-digit FedEx Air Waybill number]"**. Example: `Federal Express / AWB # 7839 2819 0291`.
* **Box 9 (Residency Info):** Provide your Social Security Number (SSN) if a U.S. citizen/resident, or passport number and issuing country if a foreign national.

### Part II: Status of Arriving Person
* **Box A (Returning Resident of the U.S.):** Check if you are an American citizen or green card holder returning from an overseas stay. State your dates abroad.
* **Box B (Nonresident):** Check if you are entering on a work visa (H-1B, L-1, O-1), student visa (F-1), or permanent immigrant visa.

### Part IV: Article Declarations (Duty-Free Exemptions)
* **Section A (Free Entry Claims):** Check **Box 1** (Tariff Item 9804.00.05) if the personal belongings were owned and used abroad for at least 1 full year.
* **Section B (Declarations):** Disclose if your parcel contains alcohol, perfume, tobacco, or commercial goods. *(Express couriers strictly enforce alcohol and tobacco taxes).*

## 5. The 4-Part FedEx Clearance Packet

FedEx Trade Networks requires a complete clearance packet containing four documents:

1. **Signed CBP Form 3299 (Rev. 05/24):** Complete and signed.
2. **Passport ID Page + U.S. Visa / I-94:** Proves legal identity and legal presence in the U.S.
3. **Flight Ticket / Boarding Pass:** Confirms you traveled into the U.S. from abroad.
4. **Itemized Baggage Inventory:** Clear list of items with estimated USD values. *(Generated automatically at [cbpform3299.com](https://cbpform3299.com)).*

## 6. How to Submit Your Documents to FedEx

1. **Email Response to Broker:** Reply directly to the clearance notification email from `@fedex.com` or `@ftn.fedex.com`. Format your subject line as:  
   `AWB # [Your 12-digit Tracking Number] - CBP Form 3299 Clearance Packet`.
2. **FedEx Electronic Trade Documents (ETD):** If preparing a future shipment, upload documents directly in FedEx Ship Manager during label creation to enable automated pre-clearance.
